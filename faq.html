Account and sign-in

How do I create a VerifyFlow account?

Open My account and choose the option to create an account. Enter your name, email address and password, then submit the form. Use the verification email sent to that address before signing in. Make sure the email address is correct and check your spam or junk folder if the message is not in your inbox.

Why do I need to verify my email address?

Email verification helps confirm that you control the address connected to your account. Verify your address before using protected account features. Open the latest verification email and follow its link. An older link may expire, so request a new verification email when needed.

I did not receive the verification email. What should I do?

First, check the spelling of the email address you registered with. Then check spam, junk, promotions and other filtered folders. Use the resend-verification option on the account page and allow time for your email provider to deliver it. Avoid repeatedly creating accounts with different spellings of the same address.

How do I reset a forgotten password?

On the sign-in page, choose Forgot password, enter the email address on your VerifyFlow account and follow the reset email. Check your spam folder too. The reset link may expire; request a new one if it no longer works. VerifyFlow support will never ask you to send your password in a chat.

Can I use the support chat when I am signed out?

You can ask the built-in instant-help assistant general VerifyFlow questions without signing in. Guest messages are not saved. To start a private conversation with a human support agent and receive an admin reply, sign in or create a VerifyFlow account first. For account access problems, you can also use the password-reset and email-verification options on the account page.

SMS verification and OTP codes

How do I buy an SMS verification number?

Open the SMS verification category in the dashboard. Choose an available service and country, review the displayed price, then continue to checkout. After payment is confirmed and the service is accepted for fulfilment, the assigned phone number and order status will appear in your Recent orders or account order details.

Where will my OTP code appear?

Open Dashboard → Recent orders or the Orders area in My account. The order details show the assigned number when available. Once the service provider reports an incoming SMS, VerifyFlow displays the verification code and the received SMS text on that order. You can use Check for SMS or Refresh service status to request the latest status while waiting.

Why is my OTP still not showing?

The receiving service may not have sent the SMS yet, the sender may be delayed, or the activation may no longer be active. Check that you are using the correct number in the app or website requesting verification. Refresh the order status. If the order expires, remains stuck after a reasonable wait, or the number is not displayed, contact human support with your VerifyFlow order number.

Will I receive the full SMS message or only the code?

When the provider returns the SMS body, VerifyFlow displays the message as well as the detected code. The exact information depends on what the provider reports for that activation. Some providers may return a code without the full message text.

Can I share my OTP with customer support?

No. Do not send your OTP or verification code to anyone, including VerifyFlow support. Enter it only into the app or website for which you requested the verification. Support may ask for your VerifyFlow order number, but never needs your one-time code, password or full payment-card details.

Number rentals, dedicated numbers and eSIM

What is the difference between an SMS verification and a long-term number rental?

An SMS verification order is intended for a specific verification session. A long-term rental is offered for a stated period, where available, and is intended to remain assigned for that rental period subject to the service terms. Review the product description, country, availability and duration before checkout.

How do I choose a rental period?

Open Long-term numbers, select a service with available periods and choose one of the durations shown on the product card or checkout screen. The price may change with the rental period. Check the selected duration and total before paying.

Where can I find my rented or dedicated number?

After the order is accepted and the number is assigned, sign in and open the order details in your dashboard or account page. Delivery information depends on the selected product and the provider response. If a paid order has no number or activation information, contact support with the order number.

What should I check before buying an eSIM?

Confirm that your device supports eSIM, is carrier-unlocked and supports the destination or coverage area of the plan. Read the plan details, validity and data allowance before checkout. When an eSIM is fulfilled, follow the installation instructions and keep the activation details private. Do not delete an installed profile unless the provider's instructions tell you to.

How do I use a mobile proxy order?

Choose a mobile proxy plan and review its plan type, duration, price and availability. After the order is fulfilled, open the order details for the connection information supplied for your plan. Keep the host, port, username and password private. If connection details are missing or do not work, request a support review with your order number.

Social Media Boosters

How do I order a Social Media Booster?

Open Social Media Boosters in the dashboard and choose a published service. Paste the public profile, post, video or content URL requested by that service. Enter a whole-number quantity within the minimum and maximum displayed on the product card. Review the estimated total, confirm that the link is correct, and continue to checkout.

What kind of link should I paste?

Paste the exact public URL for the target requested by the product: for example, the relevant profile, post, video or other content page. It should open without requiring a private login. Check the platform, username and content carefully. Private, broken, shortened in an unsupported way, or incorrect links can prevent the service from being completed.

How is the price calculated?

Social media services are priced per 1,000 units. The estimated total is calculated from the product's displayed retail rate and the quantity you enter. The server validates the product, quantity and current saved product price again when creating the order. The final amount shown at checkout is the amount to review before payment.

Will my SMM order start automatically after payment?

When payment is confirmed and the selected service is enabled for live fulfilment, VerifyFlow submits the saved service, target URL and quantity to the configured service connection. The provider must accept the order and have the service available. A supplier connection or wallet balance by itself does not guarantee completion; the service can still be rejected, delayed or placed into processing. Verify progress in your order details.

Can I change the target link or quantity after ordering?

Assume that the target link and quantity cannot be changed after checkout. Check them carefully before paying. If you submitted the wrong link or quantity, contact support immediately with your order number. A change or cancellation cannot be guaranteed once a provider has started processing.

Why is my Social Media Booster order still processing?

The provider may have accepted the order but may still be working on it. Progress can depend on the service, quantity, target page and platform conditions. Open Recent orders and refresh the status. If the order remains unchanged for an extended period or shows failed, contact support with your order number and the public target URL.

Payments, order status and refunds

When does VerifyFlow send an order to the service provider?

VerifyFlow verifies payment with the payment provider first. After payment is confirmed, an eligible live order can be queued and submitted using the product and delivery details saved at checkout. Orders made while a service or live fulfilment is unavailable may remain pending or require review. A checkout page opening or a bank debit alone does not mean that the service provider has accepted the order.

My bank says payment succeeded, but VerifyFlow says pending. What should I do?

Allow the payment status to update and check Recent orders again. VerifyFlow confirms payments with the payment provider before treating an order as paid. Do not immediately place or pay for the same order again. If the status does not update, request human support and provide your VerifyFlow order number and the payment reference shown in your receipt. Never send your card PIN, password or one-time banking code.

Why has my paid order not been delivered yet?

Payment confirmation and provider fulfilment are two separate steps. The service provider may still be processing the request, may not have accepted it, or may have reported an error. Open your order details and review the status. If it shows failed, has no delivery information, or remains stuck, ask support to investigate. Include your order number and a brief explanation.

Can I cancel an order or request a refund?

Refunds and cancellations depend on the payment status, provider status and whether the service has already started. They are not automatic or guaranteed by this FAQ. Contact human support with your order number and reason for the request. Do not submit duplicate payments while the original payment is being checked.

Where can I track all my orders?

Sign in and open the dashboard's Recent orders area or go to My account → Orders. The list shows order numbers, products and their latest available status. Some services also show delivery details or progress information. Use the refresh option for services that are still processing.

Privacy and customer support

Can customers see internal supplier details?

No supplier credentials or internal connection keys are intended for the customer-facing dashboard. Customers see their product, order status and their own delivery information. Internal supplier configuration and administrative tools are kept separate from the customer order view.

What information should I provide when contacting support?

Provide your VerifyFlow order number, the product you ordered, a short description of the issue and any relevant non-sensitive error message. For a Social Media Booster issue, you may also provide the public target URL. Never send your password, OTP, full card number, card security code, banking PIN or API key.

How does the automatic help assistant work?

The VerifyFlow help assistant provides immediate topic-based guidance for common website questions, including accounts, email verification, SMS orders, eSIM, proxies, Social Media Boosters, checkout and order tracking. It works without a paid AI subscription. Guests can use it for general questions; sign in to save a private support conversation. For an account-specific issue, payment review, refund request or a question it cannot resolve, choose Talk to customer support and an admin can reply in that saved conversation.

How will I know when an admin replies?

Open the Help & support chat while signed in. The conversation refreshes periodically while the chat is open, so new support replies appear in the same thread. You can also send an additional message in that conversation. Once the conversation has been handed to a human agent, the automatic help assistant stops answering so that the admin can handle it.

Still need help?

Sign in to VerifyFlow and open Help & support. Send your question or choose the human-support option when you need an admin to review an account, payment or order.
