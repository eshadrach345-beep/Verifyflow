Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This is a draft privacy notice and must be reviewed and completed with the operating business details before customer accounts or transactions are enabled.

Information we may collect

If account features launch, information may include account email, authentication records, orders, service usage, support correspondence and payment status supplied by the payment processor.

How information is used

Information will be used to provide requested services, secure accounts, prevent fraud and abuse, support customers, meet legal obligations and improve the platform.

Payments

Payment details should be handled by an approved payment provider. VerifyFlow should not store full card numbers or card security codes.

Sharing, retention and contact

Information may be shared with service providers only as necessary to operate the platform or comply with law. Publish retention periods and a monitored privacy contact address before launch.
