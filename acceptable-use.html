PLATFORM RULES

Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

VerifyFlow services may be used only for lawful, authorized activity. Users must comply with local law, supplier rules and third-party terms.

Prohibited activity

  • Fraud, impersonation, identity theft or account takeover.
  • Spam, harassment, threats or unsolicited bulk messaging.
  • Bypassing security, rate limits, access controls or platform safeguards.
  • Obtaining codes without the account owner's authorization.
  • Child exploitation, trafficking, terrorism or other serious abuse.
  • Using connectivity products to facilitate unlawful activity.

Service limitations

Some services may prohibit banking, financial-account or other high-risk verification. No number is guaranteed to work with a particular third-party platform.

Enforcement

We may suspend access, cancel orders or cooperate with lawful requests where abuse or violations are suspected.
